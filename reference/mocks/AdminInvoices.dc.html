How numbering works, so nobody breaks it

Sequential inside the financial year, which runs 1 April to 31 March. Allocated when the invoice is issued, in the same transaction. Never reused, never skipped. A cancelled invoice keeps its number and stays in the list.

GST is off. When the GSTIN is filled in Settings, a tax line appears here and on every invoice issued after that.
